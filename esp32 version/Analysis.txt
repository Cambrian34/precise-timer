Because I did not have a microcontroller with native wired USB HID support on hand, 
I prototyped the setup using an ESP32 over a Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) connection. 
This test successfully isolated the transport layer. The ESP32’s internal hardware 
timer executed flawlessly with zero long-term drift, proving the microcontroller 
timing logic is perfectly sound. However, the BLE protocol introduced a strict 
15-millisecond polling bottleneck, which completely eclipsed the sub-millisecond 
precision achieved on the PC side. To bypass this wireless latency, the next step 
is to source a microcontroller capable of direct, wired USB HID events.
